# Contributing to PrediMate

Thank you for your interest in contributing to PrediMate! While **PrediMate** is a proprietary product, we do allow contributions from licensed partners, collaborators, and customers.

Please read the following guidelines to understand how contributions are handled.

## How to Contribute

### 1. **Internal Contributions**
   - **Partners** and **collaborators** who have a signed agreement with PrediMate, Inc. can contribute enhancements, bug fixes, or custom features to the platform.
   - Please reach out to our support team at support@predimate.ai for an invitation to the private contribution program.

### 2. **Submitting Changes**
   - All contributions must be submitted through the formal channel provided by PrediMate Inc.
   - Contributions may involve bug fixes, new features, or integration with new hardware or software systems.

### 3. **Process**
   - Contributions should be made in accordance with the guidelines set out in the official documentation and APIs provided by PrediMate Inc.
   - We will review your submission, and if approved, it will be included in the product for all licensed users.

### 4. **Code of Conduct**
   - All contributors must adhere to the guidelines and conduct themselves in a professional manner. Inappropriate or unprofessional behavior will not be tolerated.
   - All contributions should be compliant with relevant legal and compliance standards.

### 5. **Confidentiality**
   - Any proprietary knowledge shared during the contribution process should be kept confidential, in line with the non-disclosure agreement (NDA) signed between you and PrediMate Inc.

## No Open Source Contributions

- Please note that **PrediMate** is **not** open-source software. Contributions are only allowed through the licensed partnership program and are subject to NDA and licensing agreements.
- All contributions must be cleared with PrediMate’s legal and development teams before being accepted.

## Contact Us

If you are interested in becoming a licensed partner or contributor to PrediMate, please contact us at support@predimate.ai for more information on how to start the process.

We look forward to working together to improve and evolve **PrediMate** with you!
